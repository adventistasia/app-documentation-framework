This register documents the systems, services, people, accounts, infrastructure, vendors, data sources, and operational processes the application depends on.

## Dependency types

Common types:

- Hosting/platform
- Domain/DNS
- Email/notification service
- Identity/user accounts
- Business data
- Reporting/export tool
- Internet/network
- Vendor/service provider
- Operational staff/team
- Support process
- Funding/subscription/renewal

## Review notes

- Every dependency must have an owner.
- High-criticality dependencies require impact, support path, affected workflows/capabilities, and workaround or explicit no-workaround note.
- Dependencies referenced in the Support Guide must appear here.
