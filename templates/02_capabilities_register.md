## Capability guidance

Use business verbs such as Submit, Review, Approve, Assign, Track, Manage, Notify, Report, Reconcile, Escalate, Archive, or Audit.

Avoid low-level UI features such as button, dropdown, page, screen, tab, field, database table, or API endpoint unless they represent a business capability.

## Review notes

- Every capability should have an outcome.
- Every capability should map to at least one workflow, unless marked administrative/support only.
- Role names must match the Roles and Access Matrix.
- Critical capabilities should map to dependencies and support procedures.
